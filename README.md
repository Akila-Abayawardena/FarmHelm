# FarmHelm

FarmHelm is a local-first farm management system being developed to bring crop management, farm operations, inventory, harvests, sales, expenses, and business analytics into one connected application.

## Project Vision

FarmHelm aims to provide one connected system for both farm operations and farm business management, helping keep day-to-day activity and business records together.

## Current Status

Early development — architecture and foundation stage.

## Initial Use Case

FarmHelm will initially support Scotch Bonnet cultivation. Its architecture is crop-independent, with future support planned for vanilla and other crops and specialty crops.

## Planned Areas

The following areas are planned, not completed:

- Crops and varieties
- Planting batches
- Optional individual plant tracking
- Crop treatments
- Farm tasks and scheduling
- Inventory and products
- Suppliers and purchasing
- Harvests and wastage
- Customers
- Sales and payments
- Expenses
- Capital and loans
- Assets
- Calendar
- Dashboard
- Reports
- Search
- Backup and restore

## Technology Direction

- Tauri
- React
- TypeScript
- Vite
- ASP.NET Core
- .NET 10
- Entity Framework Core
- PostgreSQL

## Development Principles

- Local-first
- Modular monolith
- Maintainable and understandable code
- Historical business data retention
- Configurable master data where appropriate
- Strong validation of important business rules
- Automated testing for important rules
- Avoid unnecessary complexity
